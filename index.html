PT Palmaraya Nusantara Tbk · Microsoft 365 Copilot Immersion

Palmaraya merupakan grup usaha kelapa sawit yang terintegrasi, dengan 7 kebun inti dan plasma yang tersebar di Kalimantan Tengah, Kalimantan Barat, dan Riau, didukung oleh 4 pabrik kelapa sawit serta 1.008 blok tanaman menghasilkan (mature blocks). Dalam sesi ini, kita akan mengambil satu masalah nyata yang saat ini sedang dihadapi divisi operasional, lalu menyelesaikannya dengan sembilan pendekatan berbeda menggunakan Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Peserta Engineering, Agronomy EPR, Agronomy MDO, Agronomy ADV, SQA, R&D Format Demo, lalu langsung praktik Bahasa Seluruh prompt tersedia dalam bahasa Indonesia

Tentang Sesi Ini

Ini bukan sekadar demo fitur. Setiap latihan menggunakan data operasional Palmaraya dan menghasilkan sesuatu yang benar-benar bisa langsung digunakan, seperti analisis, dokumen, presentasi, dashboard, atau bahkan agent yang dapat dibagikan kepada tim anda.

Key Take Away

  • Notulen rapat produksi beserta daftar action item, kemudian secara berurutan membuat dokumen Word, presentasi PowerPoint, dan workbook Excel dari satu conversation di Copilot Chat.
  • Laporan deep research yang dihasilkan oleh Agen Researcher terkait upaya mengurangi empty fruit bunch (EFB) dan unstripped bunch losses, yang diuji menggunakan data pabrik Anda sendiri.
  • Analisis dari Analyst yang memecah penurunan extraction rate sebesar 1,59 poin ke kontribusi masing-masing stasiun di setiap pabrik, lengkap dengan grafik pendukung.
  • Menyelesaikan satu proses kerja melalui Cowork yang membaca empat workbook sekaligus, membuat dashboard, presentasi, dan memo, serta menyusun draft email untuk Estate Manager.
  • Dashboard kualitas panen (harvest quality) dan daftar prioritas pemupukan yang dibuat otomatis oleh Copilot di Excel pada worksheet baru, tanpa mengubah data mentah (raw data), termasuk forecast produksi sampai akhir tahun berdasarkan pola curah hujan.
  • Materi presentasi untuk pengambilan keputusan Direksi (Board Decision Deck) serta revisi SOP yang dibuat langsung oleh Copilot di PowerPoint dan Word.
  • Membuat Agent Harvest Quality Assistant tanpa coding menggunakan Agent Builder, yang dapat menjawab pertanyaan terkait SOP maupun hasil kunjungan lapangan untuk seluruh tim SQA dan Agronomy.
  • Agent berbasis SharePoint yang membuat seluruh dokumen operasional dalam satu document library dapat diakses oleh siapa saja.
  • Dua Custom Skills dalam format SKILL.md yang membuat Copilot di Excel, PowerPoint, dan Cowork secara otomatis mengikuti standar Palmaraya, sehingga tim tidak perlu terus-menerus mengulang instruksi yang sama di setiap prompt.

  • Sesi ini tidak terhubung ke sistem Palmaraya yang sebenarnya. Seluruh data yang digunakan bersifat fiktif dan dibuat khusus untuk kebutuhan pelatihan.
  • Sesi ini bukan sekadar daftar fitur. Semua tools akan diuji menggunakan kasus operasional yang sama, sehingga terlihat bagaimana masing-masing kemampuan berkontribusi menyelesaikan masalah bisnis secara nyata.

Empat Temuan yang Saling Berkaitan

Setiap departemen hanya memegang satu bagian dari masalah. Tidak ada satu pun yang melihat gambaran besarnya secara utuh. Itulah sebabnya masalah ini bisa terus berlangsung selama tujuh bulan tanpa terselesaikan.

TEMUAN 1 · R&D

Pemupukan terlambat dilakukan. Realisasi aplikasi pupuk semester pertama hanya mencapai 62,0% di Bukit Sekawan dan 61,3% di Tanjung Lestari, jauh di bawah rata-rata grup sebesar 82,7%. Kadar kalium (potassium) pada daun turun di bawah batas kritis pada 64,2% blok di kedua estate tersebut. Akibatnya, produksi Bukit Sekawan turun menjadi hanya 11,25 ton per hektare, yang merupakan angka terendah di seluruh grup.

TEMUAN 2 · AGRONOMY EPR

Jumlah pemanen tidak mencukupi. Rasio pemanen terhadap area di Divisi C dan D Bukit Sekawan mencapai 15,8 hektare per pemanen, sementara standar perusahaan adalah 12 hektare per pemanen. Jumlah brondolan (loose fruit) yang tidak terkutip meningkat menjadi 6,56 butir per pokok, dibandingkan standar 2,0. Sementara itu, persentase tandan yang belum dievakuasi (unevacuated bunches) mencapai 23,0%.

TEMUAN 3 · SQA

Kualitas panen mengalami penurunan drastis. Divisi C dan D hanya memperoleh skor 68,1, jauh di bawah rata-rata grup yang berada di angka 85,5. Pemanen dengan masa kerja kurang dari 6 bulan memiliki skor rata-rata 73,1, sedangkan pemanen dengan masa kerja lebih dari 12 bulan mencapai 87,7. Selain itu, terdapat tiga mandor yang performanya jauh berada di bawah rekan-rekan lainnya.

TEMUAN 4 · ENGINEERING

Kehilangan minyak terjadi di pabrik. Tingginya jumlah buah yang belum dievakuasi menyebabkan antrean truk di pabrik. Untuk mengejar kapasitas, operator mengurangi siklus sterilisasi dari 90 menit menjadi hanya 72 menit. Ditambah lagi, press cone telah beroperasi selama 7.800 jam tanpa penggantian. Akibat kombinasi kedua faktor tersebut, kehilangan minyak pada empty fruit bunch (EFB), unstripped bunch (USB), dan press fibre di Pabrik Sungai Rangkat meningkat hingga lebih dari dua kali batas standar yang diperbolehkan.

Susun daily briefing untuk saya berdasarkan email, chat Teams, dokumen, dan jadwal rapat saya, supaya saya siap menjalani hari kerja ini. Kenali sendiri proyek yang sedang saya tangani, topik yang saya ikuti, dan rekan kerja yang paling sering berinteraksi dengan saya, lalu tentukan mana yang benar-benar prioritas. Susun dengan struktur berikut. Satu paragraf pembuka yang menggambarkan gambaran umum hari saya, seperti yang akan disampaikan seorang asisten ketika menyambut saya di pagi hari. Perkembangan sejak kemarin. Sampaikan informasi dari hari kerja terakhir yang berpotensi berdampak pada pekerjaan dan koordinasi saya. Hal yang perlu saya selesaikan hari ini. Tugas atau pertanyaan yang ditujukan kepada saya dan sebaiknya tuntas hari ini. Pastikan dulu apakah hal tersebut memang belum selesai, jangan menyarankan sesuatu yang sudah saya kerjakan. Email yang menunggu balasan saya. Ambil dari satu minggu terakhir, prioritaskan yang menempatkan saya di To atau CC, yang menyebut nama saya, atau yang berisi permintaan, deadline, maupun melibatkan atasan dan customer. Part C · Custom SkillsThe closing act: stop repeating the same rules in every prompt

Look back at the prompts in Task 5.1 and 5.2. Most of what they contain is not the request — it is the rules that apply every single time: never touch the raw data, rank worst first, pair every percentage with its rupiah value, caption every chart. Rules like that do not belong in a prompt. They belong in a skill.

Best for SQA · Engineering · anyone repeating similar work Time 14 minutes
Prompt, instructions, skill — three words, one idea at three levels of permanence. Think of it as asking a new colleague to do something.
 What it isWhere it livesWho can use it
PromptAsking once. You type it, you get an answer, it is gone.That conversation onlyJust you, right now
InstructionsA longer, careful prompt setting out the whole job: what to do, in what order, what to avoid, what the answer should look like.Still in your prompt, or saved in a note somewhereYou, and anyone you send it to
SkillThe same instructions, saved with a name, so you call the whole thing in one line.A SKILL.md file in a folder Copilot can findYou, and anyone you share the folder with
The important bit. The words inside a skill are exactly the instructions you would have typed anyway. A skill is not a new kind of writing and it is not code — it is your instructions, saved, so neither you nor your colleagues have to write them again.
When to use which. One-off question → prompt, do not overthink it. Something that matters → instructions. Pasting the same instructions a third time → that is the signal, save it as a skill. Want the whole team working the same way → a shared skill, or an agent (Exercise 8).

Library ini berisi laporan kunjungan kebun dan pabrik dalam format Word. Saya ingin isinya bisa dibaca sebagai tabel tanpa harus membuka setiap dokumen. Buatkan empat kolom berikut di library ini, lalu isi nilainya secara otomatis untuk seluruh dokumen yang sudah ada berdasarkan isi masing-masing dokumen. Kolom pertama, Estate. Jenis Choice. Isi dengan nama estate atau pabrik yang dikunjungi, dipilih dari daftar berikut: Bukit Sekawan, Tanjung Lestari, Muara Alam, Sungai Rangkat, Landak Permai, Rokan Jaya, Kampar Sentosa, PKS. Kolom kedua, Urgency. Jenis Choice dengan pilihan Kritis, Perhatian, dan Normal. Isi Kritis bila laporan menyebutkan penyimpangan lebih dari dua kali standar, atau komponen yang sudah melewati batas penggantian, atau kondisi yang berpotensi menghentikan operasi. Isi Perhatian bila ada penyimpangan terhadap standar tetapi masih di bawah dua kali lipat. Isi Normal bila seluruh temuan masih dalam batas standar. Kolom ketiga, Key Finding. Jenis Multiple lines of text. Isi dengan satu kalimat ringkas maksimal 20 kata dalam bahasa Indonesia, yang menyebutkan temuan utamanya beserta angkanya. Jangan menuliskan lebih dari satu kalimat, karena kolom ini harus tetap terbaca sebagai tabel. Kolom keempat, Action Required. Jenis Yes/No. Isi Yes bila laporan memuat rekomendasi dengan deadline kurang dari satu bulan sejak tanggal kunjungan, atau bila ada temuan berkategori Kritis. Selain itu isi No. Proses seluruh dokumen sekaligus dalam satu kali jalan, jangan dipecah menjadi beberapa tahap.
Perhatikan kolom kedua dan keempat. Yang Anda tuliskan di sana sebenarnya adalah aturan penilaian, bukan sekadar nama kolom. Ambang batasnya diambil dari SOP perusahaan, sehingga pengelompokannya konsisten untuk kesepuluh laporan dan tidak bergantung pada penilaian orang yang membacanya.

Copilot tidak akan mengubah apa pun sebelum Anda menekan Update. Bila muncul beberapa kartu berurutan, tekan Update pada setiap kartu sampai tidak ada lagi yang tersisa. Kolomnya baru terisi penuh setelah seluruh kartu disetujui, jadi jangan berhenti di kartu pertama.
Ada dua cara mengisi kolom ini, dan bedanya penting. Prompt di atas membuat Copilot menuliskan nilainya satu kali untuk dokumen yang ada sekarang. Autofill berbeda: ia dipasang di kolomnya dan terus berlaku, sehingga dokumen yang diunggah besok ikut terisi sendiri. Untuk pengujian di Tugas 9.3, Autofill inilah yang membuat file baru terisi otomatis. Cara memasangnya: klik panah di sebelah judul kolom, pilih Edit, lalu pada bagian Autofill klik Set up dan tempel kembali instruksi kolom tersebut dari prompt di atas. A note on terminology. This site says extraction rate, while Palmaraya's own documents and SOPs say rendemen, the term standard across the Indonesian palm oil industry. They mean the same thing, so do not be thrown if Copilot answers using either one.
The question the Operations Director brings to the meeting: "That 1.24% gap against target is worth around Rp122 billion a year. If this really can still be controlled through operations, show me where the problem is, who has to act, what they have to do, and by when it should be resolved."

Four findings that connect to each other

Each department holds only one part of the problem. Not one of them sees the whole picture. That is exactly why this has run for seven months without being resolved.

FINDING 1 · R&D

Fertiliser was applied late. First-half fertiliser application reached only 62.0% at Bukit Sekawan and 61.3% at Tanjung Lestari, far below the group average of 82.7%. Leaf potassium fell below the critical threshold on 64.2% of blocks across both estates. As a result, Bukit Sekawan production dropped to just 11.25 tonnes per hectare, the lowest figure in the entire group.

FINDING 2 · AGRONOMY EPR

There are not enough harvesters. The harvester-to-area ratio in Divisions C and D at Bukit Sekawan reached 15.8 hectares per harvester, while the company standard is 12 hectares per harvester. Uncollected loose fruit rose to 6.56 per palm against a standard of 2.0. Meanwhile, unevacuated bunches reached 23.0%.

FINDING 3 · SQA

Harvest quality has fallen sharply. Divisions C and D scored only 68.1, far below the group average of 85.5. Harvesters with less than 6 months tenure average 73.1, while those with more than 12 months reach 87.7. On top of that, three foremen are performing far below their peers.

FINDING 4 · ENGINEERING

Oil is being lost at the mill. The volume of unevacuated fruit created truck queues at the mill. To keep up with throughput, operators cut the sterilisation cycle from 90 minutes down to just 72 minutes. On top of that, the press cone has run 7,800 hours without replacement. The combination pushed oil losses at empty fruit bunch (EFB), unstripped bunch (USB) and press fibre at Sungai Rangkat mill to more than twice the permitted standard.

This is what the session is hunting for. Not one prompt in this session hands over the answer directly. Copilot has to find the connections between these findings in the available data and reach its own conclusion. The facilitator holds the answer key to verify whether Copilot's analysis matches the real root cause.

Part A · Start your day

This is the only exercise that runs on your own real working data, not the Palmaraya sample files. That means the result is different for every person in the room, and that is what makes it land.

Ask for a daily briefing on your own work

Paste this prompt as it is. Nothing needs to be attached.

Create a daily briefing for me from my emails, Teams messages, documents and meetings, so I am ready for today. Work out for yourself which projects I am involved in, which topics I follow, and who I work with most closely, then decide what genuinely matters. Use these sections. An opening paragraph describing my day briefly, the way an assistant would greet me in the morning. Updates since yesterday. Information from the last working day that is likely to affect my work and the people I coordinate with. What I should handle today. Tasks or questions directed at me that ought to be dealt with today. Check first whether they have in fact already been completed, and do not suggest something I have already done. Emails waiting on my reply. Take these from the past week, prioritising ones where I am on To or CC, where I am mentioned by name, or which contain requests, deadlines, or involve leadership and customers. Ignore automated messages and system notifications. Show only the urgent ones in a compact table, then draft replies where a reply is genuinely needed. If nothing needs a reply, say so plainly. Preparation for today's meetings. Only meetings that have not yet happened, in a table listing time and title, organiser, the topic, what I need to prepare, and one question I ought to ask in that meeting. Writing rules: plain, concise English. Name people and files specifically rather than making general statements. If a section has nothing in it, say it is empty rather than inventing something.
The point to land with participants. Not a single file was attached. Copilot read your own inbox, calendar and chats, and only you can see the result. That is the core difference from a general-purpose AI tool: it knows your work, and it respects your access permissions.

Research how to cut extraction losses, tested against your own mill

Attach both files first, then paste this prompt into Researcher.

I am the Engineering Manager of a palm oil group in Indonesia with four mills rated between 30 and 60 tonnes of fresh fruit bunch per hour. Our group oil extraction rate fell from 22.77 percent in January 2026 to 21.18 percent in July 2026, against a target of 23.2 percent. Carry out deep research to answer the following, and reconcile the external research with my internal data. Part A, industry research. What are the most common causes of rising oil losses at empty fruit bunch, unstripped bunch, and press fibre in a palm oil mill. For each cause, explain the mechanism technically, give the range of values the industry normally works to, and say which process indicators usually move alongside it when that cause is genuinely present. Cite a source for every claim. Part B, test it against my data. Open 01 Palmaraya_Losses_Mill.xlsx, sheet Losses_Sampel_PKS. For each mill, calculate the monthly average of HasilAnalisa_Persen by Stasiun from January through July, then compare it against the StandarMaks_Persen column. The agronomy behind this matters. Oil palm does not react quickly to weather. For everyone Time 10 minutes

Part A · Autofill columns Generally availableTen field visit reports become one filterable table

Ten field visit reports sit there as Word documents. To find out which estate has the most urgent findings, somebody has to open all ten. Autofill columns reads each document and writes the answers into library columns, turning a list of files into a table you can filter and sort.

Best for Agronomy ADV · SQA · IT Time 26 minutes
Check this before the session. Autofill columns is a pay-as-you-go document processing service. Your tenant needs an Azure subscription in the same tenant with pay-as-you-go connected. It costs USD 0.005 per transaction and one Word page counts as one transaction, so ten short reports cost very little. If it is not connected, run this part as a facilitator demo.
Task 9.1📁 Field Visit Reports folder11 minutes

Ask Copilot to build every column at once

You do not need to create the columns one by one. Open the Field Visit Reports folder, select Copilot on the command bar, and paste the prompt below.

This library holds estate and mill visit reports as Word documents. I want their contents readable as a table without having to open every document. Create the four columns below in this library, then populate them automatically for all existing documents based on what each document contains. First column, Estate. Type Choice. Fill it with the estate or mill visited, chosen from this list: Bukit Sekawan, Tanjung Lestari, Muara Alam, Sungai Rangkat, Landak Permai, Rokan Jaya, Kampar Sentosa, PKS. Second column, Urgency. Type Choice, with the options Kritis, Perhatian, and Normal. Set Kritis when the report describes a deviation more than twice the standard, or a component past its replacement threshold, or a condition that could halt operations. Set Perhatian when there is a deviation against standard but under twice the limit. Set Normal when all findings sit within standard. Third column, Key Finding. Type Multiple lines of text. Summarise the main finding in one short sentence of at most 20 words, in Indonesian, stating the finding and its figure. Do not write more than one sentence, because this column has to stay readable as a table. Fourth column, Action Required. Type Yes/No. Set Yes when the report contains a recommendation due less than one month from the visit date, or when there is a Kritis finding. Otherwise set No. Process every document in a single pass rather than splitting the work into stages.
Look at the second and fourth columns. What you wrote there is a grading rule, not just a column name. The thresholds come from the company SOP, so the classification stays consistent across all ten reports instead of depending on whoever happens to read them.
Copilot will ask you to confirm several times, and that is normal. The Copilot pane shows Update items cards reading something like 10 items will be updated, with Update and Cancel buttons. Copilot changes nothing until you press Update. If several cards appear one after another, press Update on each until none are left. The columns are only fully populated once every card has been approved, so do not stop at the first one.
There are two ways to fill these columns, and the difference matters. The prompt above has Copilot write the values once, for the documents that exist right now. Autofill is different: it lives on the column and keeps applying, so a document uploaded tomorrow fills itself in. Autofill is what makes the test in Task 9.3 work. To set it up: click the arrow next to the column heading, choose Edit, then under Autofill select Set up and paste that column's instruction from the prompt above. If you see no Autofill option, the service is not enabled on your tenant. Skip it, and in Task 9.3 simply re-run the prompt above after uploading the new file so the new row gets filled.

Colour-code the Urgency column

Once populated, Urgency still shows as plain text, so Kritis and Normal look identical. Yet this is the column people scan first when they open the library. The colour setting lives on the column itself, not in Copilot.

1
Click the small arrow next to the Urgency column heading.
2
Choose Column settings, then Format this column.
3
The panel offers a colour picker for each value, because Task 9.1 created Urgency as a Choice column. Pick red for Kritis, yellow for Perhatian, green for Normal, then Save.
4
Reload the library page. Urgency now renders as a coloured pill, so the Kritis rows stand out without anyone having to read down the list.
The colour lives on the column, not on the view. Everyone who opens this library sees the same colours, including on documents uploaded next month. No code to write, nothing to set up again.
Existing documents need one manual run. If you are using Autofill, select all the documents, then More options (…) → Autofill. Anything uploaded after this is processed automatically. Microsoft recommends a maximum of 10 Autofill columns per library and documents of no more than 65 pages.
Task 9.2📊 HTML dashboard15 minutes

Turn that metadata into a dashboard you can refresh

Those newly filled columns are now data. Open Copilot Chat in a new tab and ask for the dashboard.

Build me a single HTML dashboard page for reviewing field visit findings, based on the metadata columns in the SharePoint document library called Operations Documents, folder Field Visit Reports. The available columns are: Estate, Urgency, Key Finding, Action Required, plus file name and modified date. Write all the code in one code block as a single complete HTML file, with no separate CSS or JavaScript files. Lay the page out as follows. The header carries the title Laporan Kunjungan Kebun Palmaraya, the last-updated date, and a Refresh Data button on the right. Below it, four summary cards: total reports, how many are Kritis, how many are Perhatian, and how many require follow-up. Then one table listing every report with the columns Estate, Urgency, Key Finding, Action Required, and date. Colour the Urgency cell background: light red for Kritis, light amber for Perhatian, light green for Normal. Sort Kritis first. Add filters above the table: one dropdown for Estate, one dropdown for Urgency, and a search box that searches within Key Finding. All three work instantly without reloading the page. For the Refresh Data button, use the Microsoft Graph API to re-read the library column values, then update the summary cards and the table. Write the required Graph endpoint as a comment in the code, and add a short note at the bottom of the page explaining which permissions are needed. If Graph authentication is not available, include sample data in the file so the page still opens and the Refresh button still updates the view from that data. Use a clean layout with a white background, dark text, and one dark green accent colour. All text in Indonesian. Give me the result as a downloadable HTML file.
This is what management actually wants. Ten Word documents that had to be opened one at a time are now one page you can filter by estate and by urgency. You wrote no code.
Test it · upload one new report
This is the most convincing thing you can show a management team. Open the Field Visit Reports folder and upload 11 Palmaraya_Laporan_Kunjungan_Kampar_Sentosa_Agustus.docx. If Autofill is set up, wait one to two minutes, reload the library page, and the four columns will have filled themselves in with nobody typing a thing. If Autofill is not enabled on your tenant, open Copilot and re-run the prompt from Task 9.1, and only that new row gets filled. Finally, go back to the dashboard and select Refresh Data — the new report appears in the table and the summary cards update.
What should happen. The Kampar Sentosa report describes a press cone past its replacement threshold, so Urgency comes out as Kritis and Action Required as Yes. Nobody triaged it, nobody forwarded an email, nobody had to be reminded. The document arrived and the table caught up.

Create a SharePoint Agent on the document library

Step 1
Open the Operations Documents library on the Palmaraya Operations site.
Step 2
On the command bar at the top, select Copilot → Create an agent. SharePoint immediately builds a basic agent covering the whole library.
Step 3
Select the new agent, then choose Edit to open the editor.
Step 4
Rename it Palmaraya Operations Assistant.
Step 5
Under Sources, make sure both the SOP and Field Visit Reports folders are included. The maximum is 20 sources.
Step 6
In the instructions box, paste the following.
You are the Palmaraya Operations Assistant, the operational document assistant for PT Palmaraya Nusantara Tbk. You answer only from the documents in this library: the harvest and harvest quality SOPs, the mill quality and loss standards, the company communication guidelines, the board strategic memo, and the field visit reports. Answer in plain Indonesian. Lead with the direct answer, then the detail. Every answer must name the source document, and where relevant the section or clause number. Include a link to that document so the user can open it themselves. For questions about field conditions, name the estate, the division, the visit date, and the inspector. Where two documents say different things, show both and state which is more recent based on the effective date or the visit date. If the answer is not in this library, say so plainly and name which department to ask instead. Do not answer from general knowledge outside these documents. Do not assess individual performance. Where a foreman or assistant is named in a report, mention them only as context for the finding, never as a judgement.
Step 7
Fill in the starter prompts with these four: Apa temuan kunjungan terakhir di estate saya? · Berapa standar losses empty fruit bunch? · Apa empat prioritas Kuartal 3? · Kapan saya wajib mengajukan penambahan tenaga panen?
Step 8
Select Save, then Share to give the SQA and Agronomy teams access.
What makes this different from Exercise 8. This agent follows the library permissions. Someone who cannot open a document will not get answers from it, with no extra configuration. Building your own agent requires a Microsoft Copilot licence and edit permission on the site.

C · Cowork: a personalised letter for each of seven Estate Managers

One instruction, seven different letters, each carrying that estate's own numbers. This is mail merge work that normally takes half a day.

Create seven separate letters, one for each Estate Manager in the Palmaraya group: Bukit Sekawan, Tanjung Lestari, Muara Alam, Sungai Rangkat, Landak Permai, Rokan Jaya, and Kampar Sentosa. Three closing lines that always help

  • Cite the source. Every figure must name the sheet and column it came from.
  • Say so if it is missing. Where the data is not available, say so plainly. Do not invent a plausible-looking number.
  • Finish it in one pass. Complete the whole request in one go. State any assumption rather than stopping to ask.
